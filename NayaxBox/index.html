HOW TO USE THE ZENA

1. SELECT THE CHAIR

Select the available massage chair by pressing the button.

2. SELECT TIME & PAY

Select your massage time and complete payment using card or cash.

9 min — $3  |  15 min — $5

3. SIT BACK & RELAX

The massage begins automatically once payment is confirmed.

If no payment is made, a voice alert will sound and the chair will automatically adjust to prevent unauthorized use.
