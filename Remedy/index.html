Elevate your daily wellness routine with the Titan Remedy Pro 4D Massage Chair. Delivering an exceptionally lifelike massage, it transforms your home or office into a private retreat for relaxation and recovery. Whether you’re starting your day refreshed or unwinding in the evening, its advanced 4D massage technology and premium features provide a personalized experience inspired by the world’s finest spas.
4D MASSAGE
MECHANISM
Unlike traditional massage rollers, 4D Massage Mechanism intelligently adjusts its speed, depth, and rhythm to mimic the hands of a professional massage therapist. The result is a more personalized, lifelike massage that targets muscle tension with greater precision for deeper, more natural relief.
BLUETOOTH CONTROLLER
The Bluetooth controller puts effortless control at your fingertips, allowing you to operate your smartphone directly from the remote. Change your music, manage your settings, and stay connected—all without reaching for your phone, so you can remain fully immersed in relaxation.
BODY SCAN
TECHNOLOGY
Body Scan Technology uses advanced sensors to analyze your body’s shape, height, and pressure points, creating a massage experience tailored specifically to you. By identifying key areas such as your neck, shoulders, and spine, the chair automatically adjusts its rollers and massage settings to deliver precise comfort, enhanced relief, and a truly personalized massage.
SL-TRACK
SYSTEM
The advanced SL-Track System is designed to follow the natural curve of your spine, extending massage coverage from your neck to your glutes. By delivering continuous, precision-targeted relief along the entire body, it creates a deeper, more immersive massage experience that feels both natural and restorative.
3-STAGE
ZERO GRAVITY RECLINING
Originally pioneered by NASA technology, the Remedy Pro 4D features Zero Gravity positioning that reclines your body into a near weightless state for an elevated massage experience. By helping reduce spinal pressure, it promotes deeper relaxation and greater comfort from start to finish.
SPACE-SAVING
TECHNOLOGY
Space-Saving Technology allows Remedy Pro 4D to recline smoothly while requiring minimal clearance of 9 inches from the wall, making it ideal for smaller spaces without sacrificing comfort or performance.
30-CELL AIR
COMPRESSION MASSAGE
Experience full-body comfort with a 30-cell air compression system that wraps around key areas including the shoulders, arms, calves, and feet. Rhythmic inflation and deflation help improve circulation, ease tension, and deliver a soothing, spa-like massage.
LUMBAR & CALF
HEATING THERAPY
The built-in heating therapy gently warms the lumbar and calf areas to help loosen tight muscles, improve blood circulation, and enhance overall relaxation. This soothing heat feature adds an extra layer of comfort, making every massage deeper, more effective, and more restorative.

MINIMUM DOORWAY & PACKAGING
Measuring Guide
• Calculate the doorway width by subtracting 1 inch from the smallest inner width of the door frame. (1/2 inch from each side)
• Please use a standard measuring tape to measure the doorway.
• If the door cannot be fully opened, open it as much as possible and measure the width from the edge of the door hinge.
• If there is a rounded curve on the wall, calculate the inner width based on the most protruding point of the wall.
