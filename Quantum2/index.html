SPECIALIZED

Treat your feet to a spa-like experience with specialized rollers designed to deeply soothe and invigorate the soles. Targeted rolling motions help melt away built-up tension, leaving your feet feeling refreshed, relaxed, and rejuvenated.

SPACE-SAVING
TECHNOLOGY

Maximize your relaxation without sacrificing valuable room space. The Quantum 2.0 is designed to move forward as it reclines, allowing you to place it closer to the wall while enjoying a fully reclined massage experience.
